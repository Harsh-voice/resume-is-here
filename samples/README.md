# Image slots

One slot, in the "What the work returned" section of `index.html`.

To use it, replace

    <div class="shot-ph"> ... </div>

with

    <img src="samples/dialvola.jpg" alt="Dialvola call screen">

Portrait works best, roughly 1000 x 1250 px.

## Why only one slot

The other two were dropped on purpose.

- **Political campaign creative** carries a real party's marks and a real
  candidate. Not yours to publish.
- **Ads Manager screenshots** are barred by your own company policy.

Both constraints are now stated on the page itself, which reads better to a
hiring manager than a missing section would. The PK Films result is shown as
a to-scale bar drawn from figures you are free to state, not as a screenshot
and not as a platform export.

## Dialvola prompt

Your own product, so nothing is being claimed about a client.

> A clean product interface mockup for an AI voice calling service for small
> businesses in India. Portrait 4:5. Single screen on a plain near-white
> background (#f9f9f9). Shows a call-in-progress view: a live transcript in
> Devanagari and Latin script, a waveform, call duration, and a short list of
> recent calls. Minimal flat UI, thin 1px light-grey borders, 16px rounded
> corners, generous whitespace, one indigo accent (#3333cc), everything else
> greyscale. Soft even lighting, no 3D, no gradients, no stock-photo people,
> no logos, no lens flare. Editorial product-shot feel.

Add `?v=2` to the `src` if you swap the image later and the old one is cached.
