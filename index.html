The 30-day plan

Where I’d point the budget, and the one number I’d judge it on.

My 30 days would go at startup founders and developers, because this category is won on default habit: the way everyone still opens ChatGPT first even after trying Gemini and Claude. A developer who writes against Sarvam’s SDK during a 6–12 month Startup Program window usually doesn’t rewrite it afterwards, so the Startup Program is really a habit-formation product and should be marketed as one. With roughly ₹1L across three weeks I’d push the 50% V4 offer and free credits through three channels, each landing on its own page I’d vibe-code rather than routing everything through one generic signup: a comparison page for competitor-intent search on ElevenLabs and Whisper queries, leading with Indic coverage and price, with a live Bulbul demo above the fold; a creator-matched page per collab that opens with that creator’s own build so the click feels continuous; and a founders page for Meta traffic targeted on developer-tool interests and lookalikes from existing API users, running creator-whitelisted ads from their handles. Separate pages also mean clean attribution instead of guessing which channel did the work. The obvious failure mode is credit tourists, so my metric wouldn’t be signups, it would be developers reaching sustained daily API calls by day 30, the only early signal that predicts who stays when the credits end. Kill any channel above target cost per activated developer at ₹15k spend, scale what clears it.

What I’d measure

One number decides it. The other three only exist to explain that one.

  1. 01

    Developers at sustained daily calls

    Not signups. Signups are what credit tourists produce. A developer still making calls every day at day 30 is the only early signal that predicts who stays once the free credits end.

  2. 02

    Cost per activated developer

    Spend divided by that number, per channel, not blended. Blended cost hides the channel that is quietly carrying the other two.

  3. 03

    Attribution by landing page

    Each channel gets its own page, so the number belongs to a channel instead of being argued over. This is most of why the pages are separate.

  4. 04

    The ₹15k gate

    Every channel gets ₹15k to prove itself. Above target cost per activated developer at that point, it is cut. Below, it gets the rest of the budget.
